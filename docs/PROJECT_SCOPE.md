# Project scope

## Working title
**CountryLab** — Learn economics by breaking a tiny country.

## Hackathon thesis
Most AI tutors answer with text. CountryLab turns a webpage into an **experimental instrument**. Human and agent manipulate one live simulation together. The agent can inspect state, explain causal chains, and test its claims using counterfactual worlds.

## MVP
- One fictional island country
- Five regions: Capital, Farms, Industry, Port, Energy
- Four policies: interest rate, tax, government spending, tariff
- Four shocks: flood, drought, conflict, oil shock
- Six headline metrics: GDP, inflation, unemployment, debt, food, energy
- Month-by-month simulation
- Timeline of interventions
- Snapshot + restore
- Counterfactual “Prove it” experiments
- 8–12 polished WebMCP tools

## Non-goals for hackathon
- Real macroeconomic forecasting
- Country-specific real-world data
- Full agent-based population simulation
- Multiplayer
- 3D
- Procedural LLM-generated equations
- Dozens of policies/shocks

## Win condition
A judge can understand the product in under 20 seconds and see WebMCP doing something a normal chatbot cannot reliably do: reason over and operate a mutable, human-controlled live world.
